About

Kerala Map

An interactive atlas of Kerala's 14 districts and 140 legislative assembly constituencies. Constituency colours show the alliance that won each seat in the 2021 or 2026 assembly election; hover or tap a seat to meet its MLA, and switch the results layer from the map controls.

Data sources

    Results reflect the selected general election; by-elections held afterwards are not reflected in the winner shown. District headquarters are marked in district view. Boundaries are simplified for the web and are indicative, not survey-grade.

    Built with FastAPI, Leaflet and open data.
